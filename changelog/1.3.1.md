**Improved**

- Filters now apply to every section, not just Duplicates. Filter to one file
  type, a folder, a size or a date range, and Similar photos, Similar videos,
  Same song and Blurry photos narrow to match.
- Select All, Remove and the selected total only ever act on files you can see.
  Files hidden by a filter, or by switching a section off in SHOW, are never
  removed.
- Each group now has a checkbox in its header. Click once to tick every copy,
  again to tick all but the one to keep, and a third time to clear the group.
- Bigger, clearer checkboxes.

**Fixed**

- On PCs set to Windows light mode, checkboxes and the filter dropdowns showed
  dark text on a dark background and were hard to read.
