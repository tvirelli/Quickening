**New**

- Sort your results by size. The new **Sort** menu above the results puts the
  biggest duplicate groups first, or the smallest. Quickening remembers your
  choice.
- Keep overrides. In **Settings**, list the folders whose copies you always
  want to keep, in order of priority. When a duplicate group has a copy in one
  of them, that copy gets the **KEEP** badge and Select Recommended leaves it
  alone. Drag folders to reorder them. Groups with no copy in your folders use
  your normal keep rule.

**Fixed**

- Quickening could close without warning while you reviewed results, most often
  after removing files from folders that had videos in them. Video thumbnails
  and previews now load in the background, which removes the cause.
